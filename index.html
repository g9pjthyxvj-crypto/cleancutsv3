Our Story

Built for the Culture.

Clean Cuts was founded in 2018 on one belief — that a great barbershop is more than a haircut. It's a place you feel at home from the moment you walk in.

We started with one chair and one barber in South LA. Today we're three master barbers deep, thousands of clients strong, and still delivering the same standard we set day one — precision, care, and culture.
